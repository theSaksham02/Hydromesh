## Design Context

### Users
Governments, NGOs, institutions, and communities in climate-vulnerable cities. They use it to monitor street-level drainage, predict flooding, and coordinate emergency responses, especially when traditional infrastructure fails.

### Brand Personality
Optimistic, authoritative, urgent.

### Aesthetic Direction
Minimal and formal. Clean, serious, yet forward-looking. Avoid overused generic tropes (no glowing borders, excessive glassmorphism, or "AI slop").

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity and Precision:** Information must be immediately legible and trustworthy. Use fluid typography and spacing.
2. **Intentional Motion:** Use exponential easing and subtle micro-interactions to create a premium, responsive feel without being distracting.
3. **Accessibility First:** High contrast, colorblind safety, and legible typography are non-negotiable.
4. **Cohesive Colors:** Use OKLCH color spaces to ensure perceptually uniform tints and shades that feel naturally integrated.
